WHY CLOUDVERSE

Build Skills. Explore Technology. Shape Your Future.

CLOUDVERSE provides students with continuous opportunities to learn emerging cloud technologies, gain hands-on experience, collaborate with peers and industry professionals, and build practical skills for future careers.

Core Objectives

Key Benefits

Cloud Computing Foundations

Build a strong understanding of cloud concepts, architectures, services, and deployment models.

Hands-on Cloud Experience

Gain practical exposure to AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, and other relevant cloud platforms.

Industry-Relevant Skills

Develop practical skills across deployment, virtualization, storage, networking, databases, containers, security, and cloud-native technologies.

AI & Cloud Innovation

Explore AI/ML applications on cloud infrastructure and understand how emerging technologies are applied to real-world solutions.
